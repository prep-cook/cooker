Fixed

- Usage records keep xAI's charge (`cost_in_usd_ticks` and `cost_in_nano_usd`), its source and server-side tool counts, and Codex's `codex_rollout_budget_units`, each as its own dimension in the unit the upstream reported, in buffered bodies and streamed terminal events. Rollout budget units keep the upstream's number as written, fractions included. A unit the upstream did not report is left out, not written as zero.
- xAI image and video responses record their usage. A video job is charged once, at the first poll whose usage reports a charge or token count; an empty or null usage does not count, and later polls of the same job record no usage. A Responses, image or video usage records a token count only when it reports that count as an integer; an absent, null or non-numeric count is left out instead of written as zero, and never enters a derived total or the token breakdown, also when the response names a service tier. A buffered Responses body, image or video response without usage, such as a pending video poll, records no token count.
- A restart can no longer charge an xAI video job twice. Polling a job through cooker now requires that the running server created the job, or charged its usage, within the last 24 hours; a job created before a restart, through another server, or earlier gets a 404 `invalid_request_error` and nothing is sent upstream. Create the video again to get a pollable job.
- xAI video creates no longer forward the client's `Idempotency-Key` or `x-idempotency-key`. xAI documents no idempotency key for video creates, and a replayed create must not return a job a previous server process already charged.
